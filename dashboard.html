What this can't tell you

No per-game history exists outside their server. ProAvalon stores mission and vote history per game but exposes no route to read it — so who wins with whom, who reads whom as Merlin, and how assassinations actually went are unanswerable from here. Only cumulative per-player counters are public.

These aren't only games against each other. Every figure counts a player's whole ProAvalon record, including games with strangers.

Faded means under-sampled. A 100% win rate over two games is not a strength; its interval runs from roughly 34% to 100%.

Overlapping intervals mean the gap isn't real yet. Two players ten points apart at these sample sizes are, statistically, tied.
